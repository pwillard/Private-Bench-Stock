What is ecDB?

ecDB is a place where you, as an electronics hobbyist (or professional), keep your own database of the components you own: where they are, how many you have, datasheets, pictures and which projects need them.

Who & why?

ecDB was created by Nils Fredriksson (original PHP project) and later revised by Pete Willard.

This version

Version {{ version }} is a rewrite in Python (Flask) with an SQLite database, replacing the original PHP/MySQL code.
